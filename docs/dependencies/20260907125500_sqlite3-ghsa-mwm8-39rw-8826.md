## Dependency

- sqlite3 (was 2.9.5, now 2.9.6)
- Development gemspec floor now >= 2.9.6 (was >= 1)
- Lockfiles: Gemfile.lock, gemfiles/rails72.gemfile.lock, gemfiles/rails8ruby34.gemfile.lock, gemfiles/rails8ruby4.gemfile.lock
- Dummy-app and test graph only. The published gem does not require sqlite3 at runtime.

## Symptom

bundler-audit matches sqlite3 2.9.5 against GHSA-mwm8-39rw-8826. CI security jobs fail while that pin remains.

## Evidence

- Advisory: use-after-free in custom aggregate arguments when Database#create_aggregate, #create_aggregate_handler, or #define_aggregator takes two or more arguments over TEXT or BLOB values. Affected >= 1.4.0, <= 2.9.5. Patched 2.9.6.
- This repository has no create_aggregate, create_aggregate_handler, or define_aggregator callers.

## Suggested fix

Upgrade lockfiles to sqlite3 2.9.6 and raise the development gemspec floor so later resolves stay on the patched line.

## Trigger

- bundler-audit scanner match during the 2026-09-07 engineering and dependency audit.

## Assessment target

- CI and test graphs: dummy SQLite used by specs and appraisal.
- Published gem runtime: sqlite3 is a development dependency only.

## Advisory

- GHSA-mwm8-39rw-8826
- Maintainers: Medium, CVSS 4.0 score 6.3
- CISA KEV: not listed at the time of this record.

## Status

- CI and test graphs: fixed after lockfiles pin sqlite3 2.9.6.
- Published gem runtime: not_affected. Component not present in the published gem runtime. Dummy and test graphs also lack the multi-arg aggregate APIs named by the advisory.

## Applicability

- Vulnerable component present in the pre-fix development lockfiles at 2.9.5.
- Exploit conditions require custom multi-arg aggregates. Those APIs are absent from this tree.

## Priority

- CI graph: medium because bundler-audit fails the security job on version match.
- Published gem runtime: none. sqlite3 is not shipped to consumers of the gem.

## Disposition

- Upgrade. bundle update sqlite3 --conservative on the root Gemfile. bundle lock --update=sqlite3 --conservative on each appraisal gemfile. Raise the development gemspec floor to >= 2.9.6.

## Next

- Keep sqlite3 as a development dependency.
